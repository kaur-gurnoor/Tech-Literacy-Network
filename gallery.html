Cyber Smart Kids, session by session

Four 30 minute sessions for kids in grades 4 to 8, recorded over the last two weekends of August and the first of September 2026. Watch them in order or jump to the one you need. Free for families, classrooms, and libraries to use.

30 min
Recording coming soon
Saturday, August 29

Building Your Digital Shield

What to keep private online and how to lock an account down. Kids build a strong password they can actually remember, learn why details like a birthday or school name matter, and set up the habits that keep their accounts theirs.

Passwords Privacy settings Personal info
30 min · Grades 4 to 8 · Free

30 min
Recording coming soon
Sunday, August 30

Detecting Online Dangers

Fake giveaways, free game currency, suspicious links, and messages from people pretending to be someone they are not. We walk through real examples together and practise deciding what is safe to click and when to go get an adult.

Scams Fake links Strangers online
30 min · Grades 4 to 8 · Free
Session 03
30 min
Recording coming soon
Saturday, September 5

Kindness Online

What to do when a group chat turns mean. Kids talk through how screenshots and comments follow people around long after a joke stops being funny, and practise simple ways to step in for a friend instead of scrolling past.

Cyberbullying Group chats Speaking up
30 min · Grades 4 to 8 · Free

30 min
Recording coming soon

Becoming Cyber Smart Leaders

The wrap up session, where everything from the first three weekends turns into something kids can teach. They practise explaining online safety to a parent or younger sibling and leave with a checklist to walk their whole family through.

Teaching others Family checklist Everyday habits
30 min · Grades 4 to 8 · Free
