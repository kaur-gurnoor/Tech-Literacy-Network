Cyber Safety 101, session by session

Four recorded workshops covering everything we teach in person. Watch them in any order, share them with family, or use them with your own group.

Session 01
Recording coming soon

Spotting Phishing and Scam Messages

How to read a suspicious email or text before you click anything. We go through real phishing attempts line by line, point out the small details that give them away, and cover what to do when you are still not sure.

Phishing Fake links Sender checks
60 min · Seniors and adults · Free
Session 02
Recording coming soon

Passwords and Account Security

Building passwords you can actually remember, why reusing one password across accounts puts all of them at risk, and a step by step setup of two-factor authentication on a phone.

Passwords Two-factor Password managers
60 min · Seniors and adults · Free
Session 03
Recording coming soon

Privacy Settings and Safer Devices

A live walkthrough of the settings that matter most on a phone, laptop, and browser. We cover app permissions, location sharing, automatic updates, and what to turn off first.

App permissions Location Updates
60 min · All ages · Free
Session 04
Recording coming soon

AI Scams and Voice Cloning

Scammers can copy a familiar voice from a few seconds of audio pulled off social media. We show how these calls work, play a real example, and set up a family codeword system that stops them cold.

Voice cloning Deepfakes Family codeword
60 min · Families · Free
