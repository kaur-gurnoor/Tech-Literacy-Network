Team

Meet the team.

Tech Literacy Network is run by students who wanted their communities to stop losing money and privacy to problems that a one hour workshop can prevent.

Board

The people behind the workshops

Tech Literacy Network is run entirely by students and volunteers. Everyone here has another full schedule, and the programs happen because they make time for them anyway.

GK
Gurnoor Kaur
Founder and Executive Director

Started Tech Literacy Network after watching how much of everyday life moved online without anyone stopping to teach the people it left behind. Leads programming, partnerships, and curriculum.

ST
Siqi Tan
Vice Chair

Supports the executive team across planning and operations, and helps keep programs on track from one term to the next.

BY
Bereket Yohannes
Director of External Affairs

Runs marketing and university outreach, and builds the posters and campaign material behind every workshop series we announce.

AU
Atomrimam Umar
Director of Global Outreach

D
