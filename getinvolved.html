Global Expansion · Chapter Leadership

Chapter Leader

As a Chapter Leader, you'll bring TLN's free AI literacy and cyber safety programs to your own city or country, building a local team and adapting our programs to fit your community, without having to build any of it from scratch.

You won't be doing this alone. Every Chapter Leader gets full training, complete program materials, and direct support from our global team, so you're set up to succeed from day one.

  • Recruit and lead a small local team to run workshops in your community
  • Deliver TLN's core programs: AI Basics, Cyber Safety 101, and the Online Kindness Challenge
  • Adapt outreach and partnerships to fit your local schools, libraries, and organizations
  • Represent TLN as the face of our work in your city or country
  • Connect regularly with our Director of Global Outreach for support and guidance
Training
Full onboarding plus ongoing training sessions
Materials provided
Complete curriculum, slides, and branded chapter materials
Support
Direct support from TLN's global team, never on your own
Time commitment
3 to 6 hours per week
