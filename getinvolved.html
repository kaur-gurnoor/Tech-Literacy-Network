We're building our Board of Directors.

Tech Literacy Network is opening six leadership roles. If you care about digital equity and want real ownership over how we grow, we would love to hear from you.

Board of Directors

Six roles, open now

Every role below shapes how TLN runs, from what we teach to who we reach. No prior board experience required, just commitment to the mission and a willingness to build something real.

01
Vice Chair
Supports the board's leadership, helps set direction, and steps in wherever TLN needs it most.
02
Secretary
Keeps our records straight, documents board decisions, and keeps TLN organized and accountable.
03
Treasurer
Oversees TLN's finances, tracks funding, and keeps us financially transparent and sustainable.
04
Director of Programs
Leads AI Basics, Cyber Safety 101, and the Online Kindness Challenge. Owns what we teach and how we teach it.
05
Director of External Affairs
Builds relationships with libraries, schools, and community partners. Grows who TLN reaches across Ontario.
06
Director of Internal Affairs
Keeps TLN running behind the scenes, coordinating our team and keeping everything moving.
