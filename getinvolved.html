Get Involved

We're building our Board of Directors.

Tech Literacy Network is opening six leadership roles. If you care about digital equity and want real ownership over how we grow, we would love to hear from you.

Board of Directors

Six roles, open now

Every role below shapes how TLN runs, from what we teach to who we reach. No prior board experience required, just commitment to the mission and a willingness to build something real.

Vice Chair Secretary Treasurer Director of Programs Director of External Affairs Director of Internal Affairs
Role 01 · Board Leadership

Vice Chair

The Vice Chair works directly alongside our Executive Director to help set the direction of Tech Literacy Network. This role suits someone comfortable wearing multiple hats, since it doesn't come with one narrow lane.

You'll sit close to every major decision we make, from strategy to day-to-day problem solving, and step in wherever the organization needs support most as we grow.

  • Support the Executive Director on strategic decisions
  • Help set priorities across all board functions
  • Step in to cover gaps across programs, outreach, or operations
  • Represent TLN at events and partner meetings when needed
  • Help onboard and support new board members
Time commitment
3 to 6 hours per week
Good fit if
You like variety and don't need one fixed lane
You'll build
Leadership and decision-making experience
Works closely with
Every member of the board
Role 02 · Records & Governance

Secretary

The Secretary keeps Tech Literacy Network organized. Every board decision, every meeting, every action item flows through this role, which makes it one of the most important jobs on the board even though it rarely gets the spotlight.

If you're the person who already keeps meticulous notes, tracks deadlines, or has been the go-to organizer for a club or team, this role will feel familiar.

  • Take and distribute minutes for every board meeting
  • Maintain an accurate record of board decisions
  • Track action items and follow up on deadlines
  • Keep TLN's official documents organized and accessible
  • Support the Chair and Vice Chair with meeting agendas
Time commitment
2 to 5 hours per week
Good fit if
You're detail-oriented and reliable
You'll build
Organizational and governance experience
Works closely with
Vice Chair and Treasurer
Role 03 · Finance

Treasurer

The Treasurer oversees TLN's finances, tracking any funding we bring in and making sure we stay transparent about where it goes. As we grow and start applying for grants, this role only becomes more central.

You don't need formal accounting experience, just comfort with numbers, spreadsheets, and being accountable for tracking money accurately.

  • Track income, expenses, and any donations or grants
  • Maintain a simple, transparent budget
  • Report on TLN's finances at board meetings
  • Help build sustainable funding practices as we grow
  • Flag financial risks or opportunities early
Time commitment
2 to 5 hours per week
Good fit if
You're comfortable with spreadsheets and numbers
You'll build
Budgeting and financial literacy
Works closely with
Vice Chair and Director of External Affairs
Role 04 · Programs & Curriculum

Director of Programs

The Director of Programs owns everything we actually teach. That's AI Basics for Everyday Life, Cyber Safety 101, and the Online Kindness Challenge, from the curriculum itself to how each workshop gets delivered.

This role suits someone who loves teaching, building lesson content, or has run a workshop, club, or classroom before, and wants to shape what every future participant learns.

  • Own and update curriculum for all three core programs
  • Recruit and train volunteer workshop facilitators
  • Ensure every session stays accurate, current, and engaging
  • Gather feedback from participants to improve future sessions
  • Explore new program ideas as TLN grows
Time commitment
2 to 5 hours per week
Good fit if
You love teaching or building content
You'll build
Curriculum design and facilitation skills
Works closely with
Director of Internal Affairs
Role 05 · Partnerships & Outreach

Director of External Affairs

The Director of External Affairs builds TLN's relationships with libraries, schools, seniors' centres, and community organizations across Ontario. This is the role most responsible for how far our reach actually extends.

If you're comfortable with cold outreach, whether that's emails, calls, or walking into a community centre and starting a conversation, this role lets you turn that into real partnerships.

  • Reach out to schools, libraries, and community organizations
  • Build and maintain ongoing partnerships across Ontario
  • Represent TLN at community events and info sessions
  • Track outreach efforts and follow up consistently
  • Identify new regions and audiences to expand into
Time commitment
2 to 5 hours per week
Good fit if
You're outgoing and don't mind persistence
You'll build
Partnership and negotiation skills
Works closely with
Director of Programs
Role 06 · Operations & Coordination

Director of Internal Affairs

The Director of Internal Affairs keeps Tech Literacy Network running behind the scenes. While other roles face outward, this one makes sure the team itself stays coordinated, organized, and moving in the same direction.

This role suits someone who's naturally the organizer in a group project, the person who keeps track of who's doing what and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Coordinate schedules and responsibilities across the board
  • Keep internal communication organized and consistent
  • Support onboarding for new volunteers and board members
  • Track progress on ongoing projects and deadlines
  • Troubleshoot internal logistics as they come up
Time commitment
2 to 5 hours per week
Good fit if
You're the "who's doing what" person in a group
You'll build
Project management and coordination skills
Works closely with
Every member of the board
