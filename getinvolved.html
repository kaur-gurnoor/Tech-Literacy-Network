Get Involved

Help lead the next chapter of Tech Literacy Network.

We're growing our board and expanding beyond Ontario. We're looking for a Director of Programs, a Director of Internal Affairs, and Chapter Leaders to help bring free AI literacy and cyber safety education to more communities.

Board of Directors & Chapter Leadership

Three roles, open now

Whether you want a seat on our board shaping programs and operations, or you want to bring TLN's work to your own community as a Chapter Leader, there's a place for you here.

Board of Directors · Programs Leadership

Director of Programs

As Director of Programs, you'll lead and grow TLN's core programs, AI Basics for Everyday Life, Cyber Safety 101, the Online Kindness Challenge, and Cyber Smart Kids, keeping the curriculum sharp and every workshop running smoothly across Ontario.

You'll work closely with Gurnoor and the rest of the board to build new content, train facilitators, and make sure every session reflects TLN's quality and brand, whether it's delivered to seniors, youth, newcomers, or families.

  • Own and evolve curriculum for AI Basics, Cyber Safety 101, and the Online Kindness Challenge
  • Coordinate Cyber Smart Kids, our weekly Zoom program for kids ages 8 to 13
  • Recruit, train, and support workshop facilitators
  • Track program quality and gather feedback after every session
  • Work with the Director of External Affairs to match programs to partner needs
Board seat
A full voting seat on TLN's board of directors
Curriculum ownership
Shape the content behind every TLN program
Support
Work directly with Gurnoor and fellow board members
Time commitment
3 to 6 hours per week
Board of Directors · Internal Operations

Director of Internal Affairs

The Director of Internal Affairs keeps TLN running behind the scenes, making sure the board stays organized, meetings happen on schedule, and action items actually get done.

This role suits someone organized and comfortable holding people accountable, since a big part of the job is following up with fellow board members and keeping projects, like our Cybersecurity and AI Hackathon, on track.

  • Plan and run board meetings, and keep clear notes and action items
  • Follow up with board members to keep projects like the hackathon on track
  • Manage internal communication across Discord and email
  • Support onboarding for new board members and volunteers
  • Help Gurnoor keep operations organized as TLN grows
Board seat
A full voting seat on TLN's board of directors
Structure
Set the systems that keep a volunteer board accountable
Support
Work directly with Gurnoor and fellow board members
Time commitment
3 to 6 hours per week
Global Expansion · Chapter Leadership

Chapter Leader

As a Chapter Leader, you'll bring TLN's free AI literacy and cyber safety programs to your own city or country, building a local team and adapting our programs to fit your community, without having to build any of it from scratch.

You won't be doing this alone. Every Chapter Leader gets full training, complete program materials, and direct support from our global team, so you're set up to succeed from day one.

  • Recruit and lead a small local team to run workshops in your community
  • Deliver TLN's core programs: AI Basics, Cyber Safety 101, and the Online Kindness Challenge
  • Adapt outreach and partnerships to fit your local schools, libraries, and organizations
  • Represent TLN as the face of our work in your city or country
  • Connect regularly with our Director of Global Outreach for support and guidance
Training
Full onboarding plus ongoing training sessions
Materials provided
Complete curriculum, slides, and branded chapter materials
Support
Direct support from TLN's global team, never on your own
Time commitment
3 to 6 hours per week
